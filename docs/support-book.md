# The Mahjong Open — Support Book

A step-by-step guide to everything a player can do in the portal. Written from the player's point of view, so it doubles as internal support reference — if someone writes in confused about something, find it here first.

## Getting into the portal

You register on the public site, not the portal directly. On themahjongopen.com, use the registration button, fill in your name, email, phone, pick your city, choose a skill level (beginner / intermediate / advanced), and upload a profile photo — a real photo of yourself is required (no avatars, illustrations, or logos), then pay to complete registration.

After you're registered and paid, you'll get an email inviting you to set up your portal account. Click the link in that email, choose a password, and you're in.

**Signing back in later:** use the **Sign In** link in the top-right of themahjongopen.com (in the menu on mobile), or go straight to themahjongopen.com/portal/login.

**Forgot your password?** From the login page, click "Forgot password?", enter your email, and you'll get a reset link.

**"That link has expired or was already used"** — invite and reset links are one-time use. Request a fresh one (either a new invite from an admin, or use "Forgot password?" on the login page).

## Your profile

Open the account menu (your initials, top right of the portal) → View my profile.

From there you can:

- **Edit your name or skill level** — change it and it saves immediately.
- **Change your photo** — same real-photo rule as registration applies.
- **Update your notification preferences** — five toggles: table reminders (emailed before a table you're seated at), scores posted (emailed when a table you played gets scored), series updates (occasional announcements), new tables (emailed when a table opens in your city), and table filled (emailed when a table you're seated at fills up). All are on by default except new tables.
- **See your season stats** — rounds played, total score, average score, your cumulative rank, and your average rank for the current season, plus your all-time totals below that.
- **Register for a second city (if eligible)** — a "Would you like to play in an additional city?" card appears when there's another active city you haven't registered in yet. It's a separate registration and payment; use code 2NDCITY at checkout for $35. Your name, email, phone, skill level, and photo are all reused — you don't fill anything in twice.

## Finding and joining a table

Tap Schedule in the bottom nav (or the calendar icon) to see Open Tables for your city, grouped by round number.

Each table card shows the date, time, and how many seats are filled. Tap into a table to see the full details — location, notes, and who's seated.

**To join:** open a table with an open seat and tap Join this table. You'll take the next open seat.

**To leave your seat:** open the table and tap Cancel my spot. If it's within 24 hours of game time, you'll see a warning first — if no one takes your seat before the round, the host can record you as a no-show, which carries a scoring penalty.

**Add it to your calendar:** on any table you're seated at, use Add to calendar (downloads a calendar file) or Google Calendar (opens a pre-filled Google Calendar event).

## Creating a table (hosting)

Tap Create from the Open Tables screen. You'll need:

- **Round** (week 1–8)
- **Date and time**
- **Location name** (and optional address/directions)
- **Part of town** — required. See the section below.
- **Round type** — Social (light conversation, casual play), Focused (minimal talking, game-focused), or Lightning (15-minute rounds, good for a quick game)
- **Notes** (optional)

You're automatically seated in seat 1 as the table's host. Hosting means you're responsible for two more things once the round happens — see below.

**Canceling a table you created:** open it and tap Cancel this table. This cancels it for everyone and can't be undone.

## Part of town

Every table needs a **Part of town** — a short label for the area it's in, like "North," "Midtown," or "East."

It exists so players in bigger cities and regions can find tables near them instead of scrolling past every table in the area. On the Open Tables screen, tapping an area filters the list to just those tables.

**Type it in when you create a table.** As you type, you'll see what other hosts in your city have already used — pick one of those where it fits. Matching what's already there is what makes the filter useful; if five hosts type five different names for the same area, nobody can filter by it.

**You can add or change it later.** Open a table you created, tap **Edit table details**, and update the field. Tables created before this feature existed don't have one, and hosts can add it the same way.

**The filter only appears when tables have areas on them.** If no table in your city has one yet, you won't see the filter at all.

## Inviting players to your table

If your table is short a player, you can invite specific people instead of waiting for someone to find it.

**You don't have to be the host.** Anyone holding a seat at the table can send invites — so if you joined someone else's table and know a friend who's free, you can invite her yourself.

Open the table and tap **Invite players**. You'll see a searchable list of players registered in that table's city. Check the ones you want and send.

**An invite is not a seat.** Nobody gets added to your table automatically. The people you invite get an email with the table details and a **Join this table** button — they still have to tap it themselves. This is deliberate: no one ends up on a table she didn't choose.

**Seats are first come, first served.** If you invite three people for two open seats, the first two to tap Join get them. The email says so plainly, so nobody turns up expecting a seat that's gone.

**You can only invite as many people as there are open seats.** With two seats free, you can select two. The remaining checkboxes grey out once you hit the limit.

**You can't invite the same person to the same table twice.** Anyone already invited shows in the list with an "Invited" pill and can't be selected again. If she hasn't responded, follow up with her directly rather than re-sending.

**Invites always send, no matter what.** Even if a player has turned every email notification off in her profile, table invites still reach her. An invite is a personal message from another player, not a league notification, so it isn't something you can switch off.

**If an invite doesn't go through**, you'll see a message naming the person — "Couldn't reach Marcus — try again." Nothing is recorded when a send fails, so you can retry, or just text her the old-fashioned way.

**Who shows up in the list:** paid players registered in that table's city and series. You won't see players who've chosen to hide themselves from the player directory, or anyone already seated at the table. Someone who joined and later cancelled her seat *will* still appear — useful when a table's time or place has changed and she might be free after all.

**A note on where the email lands:** invite emails often arrive in Gmail's **Promotions** tab rather than the main inbox. Tell players to look there and drag the message to Primary — Gmail remembers, and future invites and reminders will land in the inbox from then on.

## Handing off hosting

Hosting a table and something's come up? You can pass the host role to someone else seated at your table instead of cancelling on everyone.

Open the table and tap **Hand off hosting**, then pick who's taking over. The table stays exactly as it is — same time, same place, same players, and nobody has to re-register.

**Once you've handed off, you can leave.** "Cancel my spot" appears for you as soon as you're no longer the host, so you can step out of that one table while it carries on without you. The table drops to three seats and anyone can take the open spot.

**Whoever takes over inherits the host's jobs** — she's the one who marks the table as played afterward and enters everyone's round scores. So it should be someone happy to do that part. She'll get an email letting her know.

**You can't take it back yourself.** Once you hand off, only the new host (or an admin) can reassign it again.

**Admins can reassign any table.** If a host goes quiet and can't hand off herself, an admin can move the host role so the table isn't stranded.

## Marking a table played & entering scores (hosts only)

Once all 4 seats are filled, the host sees a Mark as played button on the table. This unlocks score entry — a table needs all 4 seats to be marked played, no exceptions.

After marking it played, tap Enter round scores (or go to the Scores tab in the bottom nav and pick the table from the dropdown if you host more than one).

For each seated player, enter their round score, or check No-show if they didn't show up. Note: if you mark anyone as a no-show, the whole round is scored differently — no individual scores are entered for that round. Instead, every no-show player takes a −25 penalty for the week, and everyone who stayed gets a +25 bonus. That round doesn't count toward anyone's rounds-played average.

Once scores are posted, everyone at the table can see them on the table's detail page, and standings update right away.

## The official scorecard

There's a printable scorecard for keeping score at the table. Open the portal home screen and tap **Print a scorecard** — it opens as a PDF you can print at home. One sheet gives you two cards.

It's also on the **How It Works** page of the main website, so you can grab it without signing in.

The card has space for four players across four rounds, plus the bonus scoring reference — Jokerless Mahjong (+25), Self-Picked Mahjong (+10), and Wall Game (+10).

## Standings

Tap Standings in the bottom nav. There are two separate leaderboards:

- **Top Leader Score** — your cumulative score for the season: your best 7 weekly totals added together, minus any no-show penalties. Ranked by this cumulative score.
- **Top Average Score** — your average points per round played. Ranking only starts once you've played at least 5 rounds — before that, you'll see "—" instead of a rank. This is intentional (it keeps a single lucky or unlucky early round from distorting the leaderboard).

Both leaderboards show rounds played alongside your rank, and your own row is always highlighted.

## Directory

Tap Directory in the bottom nav to see your city's roster for the current series — everyone's name, photo, and skill level, plus a "Commissioner" badge for anyone who holds that role in your city. Tap any member to see their profile and season stats.

## Getting help

If something's not working, or you just have a question, open the account menu → Get help. Pick what it's about (account/sign-in, registration/payment, tables/scoring, standings looking wrong, or something else), write a message, and send it. It goes straight to The Mahjong Open's inbox, and any reply comes back to the email on your account.

## Quick answers to things that come up a lot

- **"My average shows as — on standings."** You need at least 5 rounds played before an average rank shows up. This is by design.
- **"I can't mark my table as played."** All 4 seats need to be filled first — no exceptions, even if it's just for testing.
- **"Where do I sign in?"** Use the **Sign In** link in the top-right of the website (in the menu on mobile), or go straight to /portal/login.
- **"Someone left my table and now we're short a player."** Anyone can still join the open seat before game time. If it stays empty, the host can mark that person a no-show once scores go in.
- **"I registered for a second city — do I have to fill out the form again?"** No — your name, email, phone, skill level, and photo carry over automatically. Use code 2NDCITY at checkout.
- **"Can I use a fun avatar instead of a real photo?"** No — registration and profile photos both require a real photo of you, not an avatar, illustration, or logo.
- **"It won't let me join a table."** Check whether the table is already full — a full table doesn't show a Join button, which can read as an error. Look for a table with open seats, or ask a host to invite you when a spot frees up.
- **"How do I get a scorecard?"** Portal home → **Print a scorecard**, or the How It Works page on the website.
- **"I filled a table and never got the 'table filled' email."** Everyone seated gets one when a table fills, including whoever takes the last seat. If a table filled before mid-August, no email was sent — the feature didn't exist yet.
- **"I'm hosting and can't make it."** Don't cancel the table — use **Hand off hosting** to pass it to someone else at your table, then cancel your own spot.
- **"Why can't I find the area filter?"** It only shows up once at least one table in your city has a part of town on it.
